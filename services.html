Category 1

GST & Taxation

Complete guidance and compliance support to keep your business clear of tax penalties.

New GST Registration & Modification

What it is for: It legally registers your business under the tax authorities to obtain your official GSTIN. It is also used whenever you need to update core business details, such as address or partners.

Why you need it: It is mandatory once your turnover crosses the threshold limit, and it allows you to legally collect tax and claim Input Tax Credit on purchases.

Monthly / Quarterly GSTR-1 & GSTR-3B Filing

What it is for: GSTR-1 reports outward sales and invoices, while GSTR-3B summarises tax liability, input credits and net tax payment.

Why you need it: Regular filing avoids late fees and interest penalties while keeping your business compliance record clean.

Annual Returns & Audit: GSTR-9 / 9C

What it is for: A yearly compilation of your monthly or quarterly filings reconciled against audited annual financial statements.

Why you need it: It closes your annual GST records and confirms that reported figures match your books of accounts.

GST Notice Reply & Cancelled GSTIN Revocation

What it is for: Structured replies to department scrutiny notices or revocation petitions when a GST registration was cancelled due to non-filing.

Why you need it: It helps resolve discrepancies, prevent recovery action and restore a cancelled registration so business can continue.

Individual & Business ITR Filing

What it is for: Computing annual income, claiming eligible deductions and filing ITR-1 through ITR-7 with the Income Tax Department.

Why you need it: It is a legal mandate above the threshold, provides financial proof for loans and keeps you compliant.

Tax Audit, Tax Planning, TDS & TCS Compliance

What it is for: Reviewing financial records, planning legally for tax efficiency and managing Tax Deducted or Collected at Source statements.

Why you need it: It prevents statutory defaults, helps reduce tax outflows legally and clears quarterly withholding obligations.

Category 2

MCA & Company Compliance

Establish the right corporate structure and maintain ongoing transparency with the government.

Private Limited, OPC & LLP Incorporation

What it is for: The official formation of your corporate entity through MCA, complete with PAN, TAN and incorporation certificates.

Why you need it: It creates a separate legal identity, limits personal liability and builds credibility with clients and investors.

Partnership Firm & Proprietorship Setup

What it is for: Creating legally sound Partnership Deeds or registering sole proprietorship credentials through MSME and local business proofing.

Why you need it: It establishes clear terms between co-owners, supports a current bank account and formalises operations.

ROC Annual Filing: AOC-4 & MGT-7

What it is for: Annual submission of balance sheets, profit and loss statements and operational returns to the Registrar of Companies.

Why you need it: It is compulsory for registered companies and helps avoid penalties or default and disqualification status.

Director KYC: DIR-3 KYC

What it is for: Annual mandatory verification for individuals holding a Director Identification Number.

Why you need it: Missing the verification can deactivate the DIN and block the director from managing company operations.

Director Appointment & Resignation

What it is for: Board resolutions and electronic DIR-12 filings when a director joins or leaves your company board.

Why you need it: It keeps public records accurate and legally protects incoming and outgoing board members.

Name, Capital & Registered Office Changes

What it is for: Official updates such as renaming the company, increasing authorised share capital or shifting the registered office.

Why you need it: These changes are essential when your business scales, relocates or undergoes rebranding.

Category 3

Registrations & Licences

Acquire essential legal shields, clearances and operating permissions for your trade.

MSME / Udyam Registration & Subsidy Guidance

What it is for: Registering your micro, small or medium enterprise with the Ministry of MSME to secure an official Udyam certificate.

Why you need it: It can unlock government benefits, collateral-free loans, priority lending and protection against delayed buyer payments.

Trade Licence from Local Municipal Corporation

What it is for: Official clearance from your civic or municipal body to carry out a specific trade at your commercial premises.

Why you need it: It shows your establishment meets local health, safety and zoning requirements, helping prevent sealing or penalties.

Trademark Search, Filing & Brand Name Protection

What it is for: Screening, filing and securing legal ownership of your brand name, logo or tagline under intellectual property law.

Why you need it: It helps prevent copying and gives you exclusive rights to protect your brand identity and equity.

Objection Reply & Opposition Handling

What it is for: Drafting legal responses against examination reports or third-party trademark oppositions during registration.

Why you need it: It helps prevent your application from being abandoned and supports a successful registration.

FSSAI Food Licence & IEC Import Export Code

What it is for: FSSAI supports food safety compliance, while IEC enables import and export of goods internationally.

Why you need it: FSSAI is required for food businesses, and IEC is compulsory for shipping products abroad or importing goods.

Professional Tax Enrolment & Registration

What it is for: State-level registration for businesses, employers and professionals earning income in applicable states.

Why you need it: It keeps local tax obligations in order and helps avoid state-level audits or penalties.
